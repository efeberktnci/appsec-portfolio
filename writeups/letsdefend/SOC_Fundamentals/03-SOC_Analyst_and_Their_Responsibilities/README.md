# SOC Analyst and Their Responsibilities

Course link: https://app.letsdefend.io/path/soc-analyst-learning-path

## 1) Daily analyst workflow and required skill areas
![SOC analyst responsibilities](assets/01-soc-analyst-responsibilities.png)
This screenshot focuses on what a SOC analyst does during the day. The analyst reviews SIEM alerts, decides which alerts may be real threats, and uses tools such as EDR, log management, and SOAR to support the investigation.

The lesson highlights three important knowledge areas. Operating system knowledge is needed because suspicious activity must be compared against normal Windows/Linux behavior. Networking knowledge is needed because analysts often investigate IP addresses, URLs, connections, and possible data leakage. Malware analysis basics are useful because many incidents require understanding what a malicious file is trying to do and whether it communicates with a command-and-control address.

This is the most career-relevant part of the opening SOC Fundamentals sections. It shows that SOC analyst work is a mix of alert triage, evidence review, escalation, and technical understanding across systems, networks, and malware behavior.

## Key Takeaways
- SOC analysts review alerts, validate evidence, and decide whether escalation is needed.
- Operating systems, networking, and malware basics are practical foundations for analyst work.
- Tools help the investigation, but the analyst still needs to understand what the evidence means.
